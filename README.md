# AgriSim AI — Intelligent Agricultural Digital Twin & What-If Simulator

> **"Simulate Today. Harvest Smarter Tomorrow."**  
> An AI-powered, biophysically-grounded decision-support platform enabling farmers, agronomists, and researchers to simulate alternative management decisions and quantify trade-offs across crop yield, water consumption, fertilizer usage, cultivation costs, and net profit.

---

## 1. Project Overview

**AgriSim AI** is a full-stack agricultural decision-support application and digital twin simulator. It couples validated agro-hydrological formulations (FAO-56 Penman-Monteith reference evapotranspiration, dynamic root-zone water balance, and FAO-33 multi-stage yield response) with non-linear economic and optimization models (SciPy SLSQP, multi-objective Pareto frontiers, Monte Carlo risk engines).

The platform provides an interactive dashboard tailored for Indian agro-climatic conditions and major crop commodities, accompanied by an explainable AI advisory layer operating either via local offline LLMs (Ollama) or a deterministic agronomic rule-engine.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Smallholder and commercial farmers face high uncertainty driven by volatile monsoons, groundwater depletion, escalating fertilizer prices, and climate extremes (e.g., heatwaves during flowering, mid-season dry spells). Conventional farm management relies heavily on static, calendar-based schedules or trial-and-error in the field, leading to:
- Excessive or untimely irrigation, causing deep percolation losses or root-zone hypoxia.
- Imbalanced N-P-K fertilization, incurring financial loss and soil degradation without proportional yield gains.
- High vulnerability to terminal heat stress and monsoon anomalies.
- Lack of accessible tools to test "What-If" scenarios before committing capital to seeds, fertilizers, and diesel/electricity pumping.

## 3. Goals and Intended Users

### Primary Goals:
1. **Scenario Exploration**: Enable farmers to simulate alternative irrigation methods (e.g. drip vs. flood), deficit water regimes, split nutrient doses, and sowing date shifts.
2. **Quantitative Trade-off Analysis**: Show exact numeric deltas ($\pm\%$ Harvest Yield, $\pm\%$ Water Footprint, $\pm\%$ Net Margin in INR ₹).
3. **Constrained Optimization**: Find optimal combinations of water, fertilizer, and sowing dates subject to water quotas, budget caps, and yield floors.
4. **Stress & Risk Testing**: Quantify crop resilience against heatwaves, droughts, and price volatility using stochastic modeling (Value-at-Risk 95%).
5. **Transparency & Zero Hallucination**: Ground all AI explanations strictly in verified biophysical calculations without fabricating scientific certainty.

### Intended Users:
- **Farmers & Farm Managers**: Evaluating cost-effective seasonal decisions.
- **Agronomists & Extension Officers**: Delivering data-backed guidance.
- **Agricultural Researchers & Students**: Studying crop water balance and Mitscherlich-Baule nutrient dynamics.
- **Policy Analysts & Water Basin Managers**: Assessing groundwater demand under varying climate stress.

## 4. Main Features

- **Executive Farm Intelligence Dashboard**: Real-time KPI scorecards, telemetry indicators, active farm badges, and fast tab navigation.
- **Farm Digital Twin**: Spatial field sectors with 3-horizon soil moisture profiling (Topsoil 0–30 cm, Mid-root 30–60 cm, Deep subsoil 60–120 cm), canopy NDVI vigor simulations, and virtual in-field tensiometers.
- **8 Predefined What-If Scenarios (A through H)**: Evaluates Baseline vs. Reduced Irrigation (-20%), Increased Irrigation (+20%), Reduced Fertilizer (-25%), Balanced ICAR Dosing, Early Sowing (-14d), Late Sowing (+14d), and Combined Practices.
- **Multi-Objective Pareto Analysis**: Non-dominated sorting balancing **Yield vs. Water vs. Profit**, identifying ideal trade-off compromise strategies.
- **Climate Stress Testing Battery**: Evaluates crop performance under Anthesis Heatwave (+5.5°C), 21-Day Monsoon Break, Terminal Heat Stress, and Heavy Deluge (120 mm rain in 48h), computing a 0–100 Climate Resilience Index.
- **Agricultural Risk & Sensitivity Analysis**: Tornado elasticity ranking across Rainfall, Temperature, Sowing Window, Fertilizer Inflation, and Market Prices, alongside Monte Carlo Value-at-Risk (VaR 95%) confidence bands.
- **AI Agricultural Advisor & Conversational Assistant**:
  - **7-Section Explainable Card**: Recommendation, Reasoning, Expected Quantitative Impact, Trade-offs, Model Assumptions, Uncertainties & Missing Inputs, and Practical Next Steps.
  - **Context-Aware Chat**: Multi-turn grounded agronomic chat answering farmer questions with dynamic suggestion chips.
  - **Provider Transparency**: Actively displays whether reasoning is generated by `Local AI (Ollama)`, `External AI`, or `Rule-Based Deterministic Engine`.
- **Simulation History & Multi-Run Audit**: SQLite-backed persistent audit vault allowing users to select 2 to 5 saved runs and compare performance deltas side-by-side.
- **Audit-Grade Reports**: One-click downloadable ReportLab PDF reports, daily phenological time-series CSVs, and What-If scenario matrix CSVs.
- **Dedicated Demonstration Mode**: One-click repeatable demo tour with clearly labeled benchmark data for presentations and evaluations.

## 13. AI Advisor Configuration

The AI Agricultural Advisor supports three operational modes:

| Provider Mode | Prerequisites | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **`rule_based`** (Default) | None (100% offline) | Built-in agronomic synthesis engine applying Liebig’s Law of the Minimum, Stewart water deficit, and Mitscherlich-Baule nutrient response curves. |
| **`local_ollama`** | Install [Ollama](https://ollama.ai) and run `ollama run llama3.2` | Runs locally on your machine with 0 external network requests, preserving farm data privacy. |
| **`external_ai`** | Set `OPENAI_API_KEY` or `GEMINI_API_KEY` in `.env` | Queries commercial cloud LLMs for natural language articulation while using backend calculated values. |

The application automatically checks provider availability on startup. If Ollama or cloud APIs are unreachable, it falls back seamlessly to the deterministic rule engine without throwing errors or interrupting the user.

## 14. Simulation Assumptions and Limitations

To maintain scientific integrity, AgriSim AI explicitly discloses its modeling assumptions:
1. **Evapotranspiration Assumptions**: Reference $ET_0$ is calculated via FAO-56 Penman-Monteith using IMD 30-year normal climatology or user-supplied daily weather. Local microclimate anomalies (e.g., localized wind tunnels or valley inversions) are not modeled.
2. **Soil Hydrology**: Uses a 1-dimensional vertical tipping-bucket water balance. Lateral subsurface water movement, shallow water table capillary rise, and complex 3D preferential flow are omitted.
3. **Nutrient Dynamics**: Modeled via the Mitscherlich-Baule law of diminishing returns for elemental $N, P, K$. Organic mineralization rates, nitrogen leaching dynamics, and micronutrient interactions (Zinc, Boron, Sulfur) are simplified.
4. **Crop Phenology**: Driven by thermal Growing Degree Days (GDD). Photoperiodic sensitivity and vernalization are simplified into standard stage fractions.
5. **Pests and Diseases**: The engine models water and nutrient stress; catastrophic losses from insect swarms, fungal blights, or viral epidemics are not simulated.
6. **Hypothetical Scenarios**: All stress testing and What-If scenarios represent hypothetical "What-If" explorations, **not** guaranteed localized weather forecasts.

## 15. Example Workflow

1. **Dashboard & Farm Profile**: The user opens the dashboard, inspects current farm parameters (e.g. Punjab, Alluvial Loam, 5.0 acres, Drip system), or loads **Demo Tour**.
2. **Launch New Simulation**: Opens the 8-step wizard, selects **Wheat (Rabi)**, sets sowing date to **15 November 2026**, configures a 4-event drip schedule, and inputs N-P-K fertilizer rates.
3. **What-If Exploration**: Evaluates the 8 side-by-side scenarios (e.g., Scenario B: -20% Water saves 579 m³ water with only a 3.1% yield concession).
4. **Optimization Lab**: Runs the SciPy SLSQP optimizer with a 2,500 m³ water quota to identify the optimal balanced irrigation and fertilizer combination.
5. **Pareto Frontier**: Inspects the trade-off scatter plot to choose a strategy on the non-dominated Pareto front.
6. **Weather Stress Battery**: Tests resilience against an Anthesis Heatwave (+5.5°C) and verifies the farm's 72/100 Climate Resilience Score.
7. **Risk Sensitivity**: Reviews the Tornado ranking to identify that crop selling price and rainfall variations create the largest profit volatility.
8. **AI Agronomic Advisor**: Reads the 7-section structured recommendation and asks conversational questions in the grounded chat assistant.
9. **Export & Audit**: Downloads the comprehensive executive PDF report and daily telemetry CSV.
10. **History**: Saves the run to SQLite and compares it side-by-side with previous seasons.

## 16. Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| `ECONNREFUSED 127.0.0.1:8000` | Backend server is not running | Run `$env:PYTHONPATH="backend"; python -m uvicorn app.main:app --port 8000` in a terminal. |
| `ItemExistsUnauthorizedAccessError` | Windows PowerShell execution restriction | Run PowerShell as Administrator or run scripts with `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\start.ps1`. |
| AI Advisor shows `Rule-Based Deterministic Engine` | Ollama is not running locally | If you want local LLM synthesis, launch Ollama via `ollama run llama3.2`. Otherwise, rule-based mode is 100% operational and requires no setup. |
| PDF Export fails with missing module | `reportlab` not installed | Run `pip install reportlab`. |
| SQLite locked error | Concurrent write locks | AgriSim AI connects using `check_same_thread=False` and standard WAL mode. Ensure old uvicorn zombie processes are terminated. |
